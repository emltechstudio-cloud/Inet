How to Install

1

Open Safari

iNet works best in Safari on iOS. Make sure you are using Safari, not Chrome or another browser.

2

Tap the Share button

Look for the share icon Share at the bottom of the screen (or top on iPad).

3

Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the share sheet until you see "Add to Home Screen" with a plus icon. Tap it.

4

Tap "Add"

You can edit the name if you want. Then tap "Add" in the top right. iNet will appear on your home screen like any other app.

1

Open Chrome

iNet works best in Chrome on Android. Make sure you are using Chrome.

2

Tap the menu (three dots)

Look for the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner of Chrome.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

You might also see "Install iNet" as a banner at the bottom of the screen - tap that instead for the easiest install.

4

Tap "Add" or "Install"

Confirm the installation. iNet will appear on your home screen and in your app drawer, just like any app from the Play Store.

1

Open Chrome, Edge, or Safari

iNet works in any modern browser on macOS, Windows, or Linux.

2

Look for the install icon

In Chrome or Edge, look for an install icon in the address bar (usually looks like a computer with a down arrow). Click it.

3

Click "Install"

Confirm the installation. iNet will open in its own window, separate from your browser, and appear in your applications folder or start menu.

Why install iNet?

  • Instant launch - Tap the icon and iNet opens immediately, no browser chrome in the way.
  • Native feel - Full-screen experience with push notifications, just like WhatsApp or Signal.
  • Always available - Works offline for reading past messages. Auto-reconnects when you are back online.
  • Private by design - Your SIM file stays on your device. Nothing is stored on our servers.
