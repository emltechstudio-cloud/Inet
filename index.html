Why iNet?

Built for privacy. Built for people. Built to be simple.

Digital SIM

Your identity is a 6-digit PIN, not a phone number. Create it in seconds. Use it anywhere.

Private Chat

End-to-end encrypted messaging. Photos, videos, voice notes, and stickers. No logs kept.

Audio & Video Calls

Peer-to-peer calls with no middleman. Group calls with up to 8 people. Crystal clear quality.

Group Rooms

Create a room, share the link, and anyone can join. No account needed for guests.

No Data Stored

Your SIM file is encrypted and stays on your device. Messages are peer-to-peer or stored only until delivered.

Push Notifications

Get notified for calls, messages, and group invites - even when the app is closed.

How it works

Three steps. No forms. No verification. Just you.

1

Get your SIM

Tap "Get New SIM" and choose a password. iNet generates a unique 6-digit PIN and a device ID. Your SIM is encrypted and saved as a file you can download.

2

Add contacts or dial

Save contacts by name and PIN, or use the dialpad to call any 6-digit number directly. If they are on iNet, the call connects instantly.

3

Chat and call

Send messages, make audio or video calls, create group rooms. Everything is end-to-end encrypted. No phone number, no email, no tracking.

Build with iNet

Add "Continue with iNet" to your app. Send OTPs. Push messages to users. Use iNet as the identity layer for your platform - no Google, no email, no passwords.
