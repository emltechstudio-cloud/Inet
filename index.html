Built for Privacy, Designed for You

Your Identity, Your Control

Three ways to access your account: device, Flink SIM, or password. No email required, no phone number needed.

No Permanent Cloud Storage

Messages, calls, and media stay on your device. We don't store your conversations in the cloud.

WebRTC Powered

Voice and video calls use direct peer-to-peer connections. Once connected, the backend steps out.

Group Communication

Create groups with Flink numbers. Voice, video, and chat - all with the same privacy-first approach.

Web Push Notifications

Get notified of incoming pings and calls, even when the app is closed.

Link-Based Rooms

Create shareable rooms for video, voice, or chat. Guests can join without a Flink number.

What Makes Flink Different

We intentionally sacrifice some convenience for privacy and simplicity.
